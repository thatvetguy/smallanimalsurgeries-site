Veterinarian-built

A small studio with clinic-floor instincts.

Small Animal Surgeries, Inc was founded by a small animal veterinarian with emergency practice experience and a habit of building practical mobile tools. The company name started in veterinary medicine, but the portfolio has grown into a toolkit for students, clinicians, and independent workers.

Operating Principles

Practical first. Polished because it matters.

Offline where it matters

Reference and study tools should keep working in clinics, barns, schools, and bad Wi-Fi.

Clear boundaries

Veterinary apps inform and organize; they do not replace veterinary care or prescribe treatment.

Small enough to care

Each product is built around a specific job, then refined until the common path feels fast.

Privacy by restraint

Collect less, ask for less, and avoid accounts unless they genuinely help the user.
